# Acquisition Gate

- [ ] ICP/channel/message/offer/CTA fit.
- [ ] Manual method and frequency defined.
- [ ] Cost and effort are explicit.
- [ ] Metric, success and abandon criteria exist.
- [ ] Organic/low-cost channels considered first.
- [ ] Outreach is relevant, opt-out respectful and not automatic.
- [ ] No purchased lists, spam or invented proof.
