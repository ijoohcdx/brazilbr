# Validation Gate

- [ ] Problem/urgency tested with more than opinion where feasible.
- [ ] ICP and acquisition hypothesis have evidence.
- [ ] Willingness to pay is tested or explicitly unknown.
- [ ] Opportunity and competition are bounded without invented precision.
- [ ] Risks and disconfirming evidence are recorded.
- [ ] Human owner chose Go, No-Go or Pivot.
- [ ] Development is blocked unless Go or approved Pivot.
