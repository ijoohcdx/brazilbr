# CRO Gate

- [ ] Immediate what/for whom/problem answer.
- [ ] One primary CTA and clear next action.
- [ ] Demonstration is truthful and not fabricated.
- [ ] Problem, solution, benefits and differentiation are distinct.
- [ ] Pricing is current and labeled as hypothesis if needed.
- [ ] FAQ handles objections without unsupported claims.
- [ ] Mobile-first, accessible, fast and form states tested.
