# Launch Checklist

- [ ] Product decision is Go and owner recorded.
- [ ] Core flow works for new and existing users.
- [ ] Critical/High security issues resolved or explicitly approved.
- [ ] Build/lint/tests pass and commit is known.
- [ ] Environment names verified; no secrets in repo/logs.
- [ ] Auth, database and Rules are enabled/published as evidenced.
- [ ] Domain and SPA/404 behavior tested.
- [ ] Mobile/desktop smoke passed.
- [ ] Landing, CTA, pricing and claims reviewed.
- [ ] SEO metadata, sitemap, robots and schema checked.
- [ ] Acquisition offer/manual outreach approved; no spam.
- [ ] Cost/billing owner and alerting defined.
- [ ] Monitoring/error handling and incident owner defined.
- [ ] Backup/export and rollback procedure tested or limitation accepted.
- [ ] Human launch approval recorded.
